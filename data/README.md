# Example reading lists

CSV columns, quoting rules, and an example row are documented in the app: open `index.html` and expand the format panel under **Load your reading list**.

This directory holds sample files you can paste, choose locally, or load by URL:

- [`japanese-books.csv`](./japanese-books.csv) — the original DeckiReader sample
- [`english-books.csv`](./english-books.csv) — reading history extracted from `layout: review` posts on jarrodparkes.com. Page counts and ISBNs are from typical Amazon/publisher hardcover or standard print editions. Difficulty and genre are inferred, not taken from the original reviews. Valve’s employee handbook and Basecamp’s *Shape Up* have no commercial ISBN.
